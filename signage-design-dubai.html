Signage Design

Overview

Where the brand meets the street.

Signage is an identity built at architectural scale. Legibility, viewing distance, lighting and material behaviour matter as much as the drawing. Fascia, wayfinding and environmental graphics are developed as one coordinated scheme, with specifications prepared for fabrication.

Why it matters: Signage is read at a glance, from a distance, often in motion — it has to work under different constraints than screen or print design.

What's Included
  • Fascia and building signage
  • Retail and interior signage
  • Wayfinding systems
  • Environmental branding
  • Exhibition and event graphics
Frequently Asked

Common questions

Do you provide fabrication-ready specifications?

Yes, drawings are prepared with the measurements and material notes a fabricator needs.

Both — from a single fascia sign to a coordinated wayfinding scheme across a site.
