Branding Design

Overview

Identity systems built to stay consistent long after the launch.

A logo is the smallest part of an identity. We build the system around it — typographic hierarchy, colour, imagery, layout grids and the rules that govern how they meet. Everything is delivered as working files and written guidelines, so the brand can be applied correctly by any team without returning to us for every decision.

Why it matters: A brand that holds together across every touchpoint reads as more deliberate, and is faster for any team to apply correctly.

What's Included
  • Brand identity and logo systems
  • Visual identity systems
  • Corporate stationery
  • Brand guidelines
  • Rebranding and brand refinement
Frequently Asked

Common questions

Do we get the working files, not just a logo?

Yes. Delivery includes source files and a written guideline document, not a flattened logo pack.

Can you work from an existing identity?

Yes — rebranding and refinement projects start from what already exists rather than replacing it outright.
