Screen Printing

Overview

Brand identity applied to what people wear.

Screen printing gives dense, durable colour on fabric. Identity artwork is adapted for textile rather than reused from print files, accounting for garment colour, ink behaviour and wash performance. Uniforms, event apparel and merchandise are held to the same standard as everything else.

Why it matters: Artwork prepared specifically for textile holds up better under wash and wear than print files reused as-is.

What's Included
  • T-shirt and apparel printing
  • Uniforms and workwear
  • Textile and fabric printing
  • Promotional and event apparel
