Who We Are

About KiteCalls

KiteCalls is a Dubai branding agency connecting strategy, design, digital marketing and physical production under one studio. As a branding and marketing agency in Dubai, we work across brand identity design, graphic design, packaging design, signage design, social media design, website design and printing services — so a single team carries a project from the first idea through to the finished, delivered piece.

Consistency across every brand touchpoint — digital and physical — is treated as the point of the work, not a nice-to-have added at the end.

Our Approach

How the work moves.

Five stages on one continuous line. Each hands over to the next without losing the original idea.

01
Strategy

We establish what the brand stands for, who it speaks to and where it competes. Every decision that follows is measured against this.

02
Creativity

Concepts are developed as ideas first, not layouts. We look for the one that is simplest to defend and hardest to imitate.

03
Design

The idea becomes a system: typography, colour, imagery and layout, resolved once and then applied everywhere.

04
Production

Files are prepared for the medium they will live in — press, substrate, screen or surface — and made in our own facilities.

05
Marketing

The finished brand is put to work through campaigns, content and channels chosen for the audience it needs to reach.
